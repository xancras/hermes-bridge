Privacy policy

hermes-bridge · Last updated 14 November 2026

Who this applies to

hermes-bridge is a personal tool with a single user: its owner, who also built and runs it. It is not offered to anyone else, and no other person's Google account is connected to it.

Google data it accesses, and why

PermissionUsed for
Gmail, read-only
gmail.readonly		Reading new messages to find requests, deadlines and dates. It cannot send, delete or change email.
Calendar, read-only
calendar.readonly		Seeing existing events to avoid clashes.
Calendars it creates
calendar.app.created		Creating one separate calendar and adding events to it, only after the owner approves each one. It cannot change the owner's other calendars.
Tasks
tasks		Adding tasks to its own task list, only after the owner approves each one.
Drive, app files only
drive.file		Storing encrypted backups in a folder it created. It cannot see any other Drive files.

How the data is used and stored

  • All processing happens on the owner's own computer, using AI models that run locally on that machine. Google data is not sent to any third-party AI service.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored on that computer, readable only by a dedicated system account.
  • Short, cleaned excerpts of new emails are kept on that computer until the owner has dealt with them.
  • Backups are encrypted on the owner's computer (AES-256) before being uploaded to the owner's own Google Drive.

Sharing

Google data is not sold, shared, or transferred to anyone. It is not used for advertising, and it is not used to train AI models.

hermes-bridge's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Email excerpts are deleted once handled. Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; the owner can delete the local data and the Drive backup folder at any time.

Contact

Questions: use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
