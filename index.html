hermes-bridge

A private, single-user tool. Not offered to the public.

hermes-bridge connects its owner's own Google account to a personal assistant that runs entirely on the owner's own computer, using locally hosted AI models. It has one user: the person who built it.

What it does

  • Reads new email (read-only) to spot requests, deadlines and dates, then asks the owner whether each one should become a task, a calendar event, or be ignored.
  • Reads the owner's calendars (read-only) to avoid clashes.
  • Adds events only to a separate calendar that it created itself, and adds tasks only to its own task list. It never edits or deletes the owner's existing events.
  • Stores its own encrypted backups in a folder it created in the owner's Google Drive.

It never sends, deletes or modifies email.

Privacy policy

hermes-bridge is a personal project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.
